STRIKEPOINT

Attack or defend two sites on Foundry. Plant the charge, hold the angles, win rounds, earn credits, buy better guns. Every kill in a round raises the stakes — five in one round is an ACE.

Controls

  • WASD Move · Shift Walk (silent) · Ctrl Crouch · Space Jump
  • LMB Fire · RMB Aim down sights · R Reload
  • 1 Primary · 2 Sidearm · 3 Knife · Q Last weapon · Scroll Cycle
  • 4 Charge (attack) · E Hold to plant / defuse · G Drop weapon
  • B Buy menu (buy phase) · Tab Scoreboard · M Toggle minimap
  • Esc Pause / release mouse

Settings

Click Deploy, then click the game to lock your mouse. Sound starts on first click. Desktop browser recommended (Chrome, Edge, Firefox).
